**Design:** Each message carries a unique `id` and `attempt` counter. On failure, retry with exponential backoff and jitter, capping at N attempts. Worker tracks processed `id`s (dedupe store) and final states.

**Invariants:**
1. At-most-once side effects (idempotent handling)
2. Retries bounded (≤N)
3. At-least-once delivery to DLQ after exhaustion
4. Deterministic outcome per id

**Edge cases to test:**
- Crash between processing and dedupe write
- Duplicate delivery mid-retry
- Clock skew/jitter collisions
- DLQ full or write fails
- Attempt counter reset on lease expiry
- Concurrent workers racing same id
- Partial success before failure
